# Published epochal comparison dataset

**Secondary source:** Susanta Barik, “Computational Efficacy of Pathani Samanta’s Astronomy,” *Central India Journal of Historical and Archaeological Research*, Vol. XI/XII, No. 36/37 (2021–2022), pp. 62–69. The epochal mean-position table is on pp. 66–67.

**Source PDF:** https://cijhar.in/pdf/2021_Vol_11_No_36_Vol_11_No_37.pdf

The paper states that Samanta's epoch is 12 April 1869 at mean sunrise at Lanka. The data file stores the Siddhanta Darpana and “Modern” columns from the paper after converting degrees/minutes/seconds to decimal degrees.

## Limitations

- These are values as reported by a secondary paper, not a fresh ephemeris query.
- “Mean positions” must not be compared directly with apparent/topocentric JPL output without harmonising conventions.
- The original Sanskrit edition and exact verse/table references still need verification.
- Jupiter's modern entry is ambiguous in extracted text and is intentionally omitted.
- No uncertainty estimates are supplied in this transcription; do not infer precision beyond the printed values.
